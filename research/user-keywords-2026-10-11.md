## Metric interpretation

Distinguish 未收录 / 没有获得搜索量数据 / 实际搜索量为零 / Google Trends 相对热度为零. These four states are not interchangeable. Do not fill missing KD, turn unknown search volume into zero, call this historical SERP current, label inferred competition as measured, or change original numbers without evidence.

## User trend evidence and limitations

The user's prior Google Trends screenshot showed a pronounced rise at the end of the observation window. The window may include a dotted forecast segment. Stability and future game lifecycle are unknown. Peak 100 cannot be converted into an exact monthly volume. JoJo-related search volume cannot be counted as demand for the Roblox game. Recheck 7-day and 30-day trends if available; compare the main term with GPTs if data is available. Preserve this baseline and record new findings separately.

## User competition conclusions

- Main term / Wiki: KD 47.7, medium difficulty; YouTube and TikTok appear near the front; no dedicated Wiki clearly dominates. There may be an early independent-Wiki opportunity.
- Stand acquisition: related videos exist; no targeted text guide was found. Priority candidate, but continuing player demand needs verification.
- Codes: KD 46.7, relative peak 58, professional publishers have already posted, about five strong positions. Do not rely on codes alone.
- Stands: KD 41.2, relative peak 3, old JoJoWiki/Fandom/CBR content occupies results, about six strong positions. This does not establish that game-specific demand is satisfied.
- Tier list: KD 43, relative peak 0, TierMaker holds some results. No sufficient reason to prioritize a ranking page.
- Trello: KD 34.8, relative peak 1, possibly weaker competition; demand uncertain. Verify an actual official or community board.
- Discord: a community-shared invite exists; do not call it official without verification.
