## What are the two ways to get your first Stand?

Your first Stand comes from a qualifying fight knockout or a Stand Arrow. The game’s **Encyclopedia → Stands** entry, readable in [Abitsane’s footage at 0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=taOqWP56Mrk&t=10s), documents both methods. You begin without a Stand, and the result is described as random by rarity.

| Method | Requirement shown in the Encyclopedia | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fight awakening | Another fighter knocks you out in a real fight | A 1-in-10 chance, with one chance per minute |
| Stand Arrow | Hold the Arrow until compatibility reaches 100% | Awakening described as certain at full compatibility |

## How does the fight method work?

Take part in a real fight and be knocked out by another fighter. The filmed Encyclopedia gives that qualifying knockout a **1-in-10 awakening chance**, subject to **one chance per minute**. A knockout is an opportunity to awaken; it does not guarantee a Stand.

1. Open the game’s menu and read **Encyclopedia → Stands → How to get a Stand**.
2. Use a real fight involving another fighter rather than resetting or falling.
3. If the knockout does not awaken a Stand, respect the documented one-minute limit before another qualifying opportunity.

The Encyclopedia explicitly excludes character resets, falls, and a tap when you have 1 health. This explains why repeatedly resetting your character is not a Stand route. The odds here come from the visible game text; no probability testing was performed by this wiki.

## How do you use a Stand Arrow?

Obtain the Stand Arrow and hold it until its **compatibility reaches 100%**. The Encyclopedia describes full compatibility as a certain awakening. [SuperZydan’s separate gameplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9rdSW-8ULE&t=50s) shows an Arrow contest, followed by a reported Stand awakening later in the clip.

1. Watch for the Stand Arrow event described in the checked player footage.
2. Look for the Arrow. Abitsane describes a purple outline at [0:27–0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=taOqWP56Mrk&t=27s).
3. Keep possession while compatibility fills, following your current game’s prompts.
4. Use the **100% compatibility condition**, rather than an assumed number of seconds, to judge completion.

Other players can compete for the Arrow, as the second clip demonstrates. A fixed spawn location, event interval, interaction key, or exact holding time has not been verified. The available footage supports the event and possession route; it does not support a guaranteed map circuit.

## Can you choose or change the Stand you get?

The Encyclopedia describes awakening as random by rarity. It also describes rerolling to a different Stand and says the current Stand is excluded from that reroll. This does not establish the full roster, rarity percentages, strongest Stand, or how reroll currency is earned.

Check the current menu’s cost before spending any currency. This guide does not label a reroll as free or provide a tier list without combat evidence.

## Do you need a code to awaken?

Neither documented method requires a code. The first video mentions codes, but this wiki has not verified a working code, its reward, or successful redemption. You can follow the Encyclopedia’s fight or Arrow method without relying on a claimed code reward.

Use the [developer and community resources](/resources/community-links/) for announcements, and the [release information](/resources/release-information/) for the availability check. No daily code-update promise is made.
