## Where does the Discord invite lead?

The invite **discord.com/invite/moriohhigh** resolved through Discord's public invite API to a server named **Morioh High** on October 11, 2026. Game-related pages, including BloxInformer and MrGuider, share that invite.

This confirms the destination at the check time. A matching name and several links do not establish who controls the server. The Roblox social-links API required authorization, so this wiki has not independently verified a developer-owned Discord link.

  This invite is listed as a community resource. It is not presented as a developer-endorsed or Roblox-endorsed server. Invites can change or expire; confirm announcements through the developer group when provenance matters.
</Callout>
